Your Platform

List what helps you reach readers, such as your work experience, newsletter, social media following or speaking events. Give numbers where you can.

Chapter Overview

Summarize each chapter in a sentence or two. Some agents ask for this. If yours doesn’t, you can skip this step.

Author Bio

Mention writing credits, relevant experience or anything that connects you to the book. One to three sentences is enough. If you don’t have writing credits, one sentence about yourself is fine.

Salutation & Closing

Address the agent by name, and check the spelling.

Advice Mistakes to avoid and what to do next

Length

Aim for 250 to 350 words, with 150 to 200 of them on the pitch. If your letter is over 400 words, shorten the pitch.

Be specific

Vague pitches all sound alike. Use details that only your book has.

✗ “She must investigate the strange circumstances surrounding the murder.”
✓ “She must find out why every victim has insect larvae burrowed into their skulls before the killer adds her name to the list.”

Cut

  • Statements about theme, such as “a story about grief”
  • Rhetorical questions
  • Flattery aimed at the agent
  • Comparisons to huge bestsellers
  • Apologies of any kind, such as for being a new writer

Before you send

  • Read archived queries on Query Shark.
  • Find agents on QueryTracker and Manuscript Wishlist.
  • Finish the manuscript and polish the first ten pages.
  • Ask someone who hasn’t read your book to read the query and tell you if anything is unclear.
  • More: word counts by genre, blurbs vs. query pitches, AgentQuery’s guide.
