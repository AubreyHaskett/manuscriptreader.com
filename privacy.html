Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Everything on this site runs in your browser. Your writing and the files you open here are never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared.

What we collect

Anonymous page-visit counts, through GoatCounter. It records that a page was visited, not who visited or what they typed. It uses no cookies, doesn’t store IP addresses, and doesn’t follow you between visits. Here is GoatCounter’s privacy policy.

What we don’t collect

  • Your manuscript, query drafts, or anything else you type
  • The PDF or Word files you convert, the cover images you add, and the EPUB files you download
  • The audio you generate, which stays in memory until you close the tab
  • Accounts (there are none)
  • Cookies or tracking IDs

If you send feedback

The feedback form is the only thing that sends data from this site. When you use it, your message, your email address if you add one, and basic details about your device (operating system, browser, and screen size) are sent to us through FormSubmit. We use this only to reply to you and fix problems. To have your feedback deleted, send another message asking us to delete it.

What stays in your browser

A few things are saved in your own browser so they’re there next time: your query draft, your Prose Analysis settings and word list, your Convert to EPUB settings, your audio engine choice, your light or dark setting, and your Read Aloud settings if you choose to keep them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

While a tab is open, the text you’re working on is also kept so a refresh doesn’t lose it. Closing the tab clears it. None of this leaves your device.

Other services

Your browser also downloads fonts from Google Fonts, the Kokoro voice model from a public CDN, and the code libraries that read PDF and Word files and build EPUB files from cdnjs. The model is cached after your first visit. None of these services ever receive your text or your files. There are no ads, social trackers, or session recordings.

Changes

If the way this site handles data changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

Questions? Use the feedback form at the bottom of any page.

If this helps your writing, you can buy me a coffee.
