PDFs include page numbers and other text that repeats on every page. These options remove that text so it doesn't show up in the middle of your story.
Removes the number printed at the top or bottom of each page.
Removes text that repeats at the top or bottom of each page, such as the book title or the author's name. This is sometimes called a running header.
Use this to leave out pages such as the title page, the copyright page or a sample chapter at the end. Count pages by their position in the PDF, not by the numbers printed on them.

Automatic finds chapters by their headings, including large or bold text, Word heading styles and lines like “Chapter 1”. Only “Chapter 1”-style lines finds only lines that start with Chapter, Part, Prologue or a similar word. Use it if Automatic marks headings that aren’t chapters. Either way, you can correct the chapter list after converting.
A scene break is the pause between two scenes, usually marked with * * * or a blank line. Symbols like * * * and ### are found automatically. This setting controls how scene breaks look in your e-book.
Turn this on if your book separates scenes with an empty line instead of a symbol.
Printed books usually indent the first line of each paragraph. Websites usually put space between paragraphs. You can choose either one, or both.
Without this, a paragraph that continues onto the next page is split in two.
