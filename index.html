Free tool, running entirely on your device, so audio quality and speed depend on your hardware. Works best on a Mac in Chrome. That's the price of free. If generation is slow or the voice sounds garbled, your hardware likely can't keep up with the Kokoro model; try a different computer or browser.

Voices are generated by Kokoro, an open-source text-to-speech model trained on permissively licensed audio. We didn't build Kokoro, we just use the open-source library.
